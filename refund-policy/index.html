Refund Policy

Setup fees

The setup fee is due before kickoff and is non-refundable once work has started.

Guarantee

If the agreed result in the scope of work is not reached by day 45, Next Lever continues working at no additional charge until it is, provided the client has delivered the access, approvals, and walkthroughs described in the scope.

Monthly management

Optional month-to-month management can be cancelled by either side with 30 days' written notice. No refunds on months already delivered.

Disputes

Contact us first — we revise deliverables before anything else. Refund requests outside the guarantee are reviewed case by case.
