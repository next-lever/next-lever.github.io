Every engagement has a written scope of work attached to the invoice. Payment of the setup invoice confirms the scope. Changes to scope are agreed in writing before work starts on them.

Payment

Setup fees are due before kickoff. Work starts once payment is received. Optional monthly management is billed month to month and can be cancelled by either side with 30 days' written notice.

Timelines depend on the client providing the access, approvals, and walkthroughs described in the scope. Delays on the client side move the timeline by the same amount.
